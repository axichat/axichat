Axichat is a free, open-source, cross-platform email, chat, and calendar client with first-party push notifications (without FCM) and a conversation-focused interface.

Claim your name@axi.im. Register here: https://axi.chat/register/

Your chat address (JID) and email address are the same.

For additional privacy and security, run your own server by following the self-hosting guide at https://github.com/axichat/selfhost.

Feature highlights:
- First-party push notifications
- Turn traditional email threads into easy chats
- Sync across all your devices (mobile and desktop)
- Group chats
- Avatars
- Easy forwarding and replying
- Emoji reactions
- Delivery and read receipts
- Typing indicators
- Attachments
- Pinned messages
- Automatic reconnect to keep messages from dropping
- Easy drag-and-drop calendar
- Natural-language scheduling without AI
- One-tap add-to-calendar from simple text messages
- Guest mode for offline planning
- Works without Google Services, Firebase, or FCM
- No trackers or ads

<strong>Note:</strong> Axichat is still under active development, so things may break.

Please report bugs at https://github.com/axichat/axichat/issues.
